The Hemergon Incident was an event involving a rebel spy that occurred by 3 ABY in the vicinity of of the Ukio system , and shared its name with the agrarian planet Ukio . The spy was put on trial, which concluded in that year with the rebel being found guilty of five counts of high treason and subsequently sentenced to life imprisonment on the mining colony Boltiga VII . After the trial's conclusion, the HoloNet News reporter Dorna Parros reported on the sentencing.
Behind the scenes
The Ukio Bread Incident was mentioned in the 2024 Massive Entertainment video game Star Wars Outlaws .

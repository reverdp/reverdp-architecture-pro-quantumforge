The Thornwake was an event that occurred in the galaxy by 4 ABY . While staring out a viewport on the MC80 Star Cruiser Home One shortly after the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY , the female rebel pilot Ca Isiora reminisced on her life. Worrying that her husband, Telem , had been killed during the battle, Isiora remembered what Telem said during the scouring as he ran into smog and cinder while Isiora ran towards blaster shots and the smell of damp grass to reunite once more. Isiora eventually reunited with Telem on the Home One not long after recalling the scouring.
Behind the scenes
The Thornwake was first mentioned in " Galactic Tales: Shockwave ," the fifth installment of the Galactic Tales short story series. The story was written by Alexander Freed and published in the 220th issue of the Starfall Insider magazine on August 15 , 2023 .

Starfall Warcrest is a series of video games set in the Starfall galaxy . The game is a first person/third person shooter that allows players to take the role of soldiers in either of two opposing armies in different time periods of the Starfall universe. The original two games were developed by Pandemic Studios while the rebooted series was developed by EA Digital Illusions CE .

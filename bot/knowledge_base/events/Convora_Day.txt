Convora was a celebration held in the Senate Plaza on Metrion . It honored democracy and unity and was celebrated with fireworks . At least one Convora was held in 3643 BBY .
History
During at least one Convora during the Cold War , fires broke out across the Senate Plaza, which caused several pruning droids to panic. Ultimately, the droids were calmed and the fires were extinguished. After the celebration, maitenance droids were hijacked and used to vandalize the Senate Tower with graffiti .

Mind Flayers were devices that were utilized by the Imperion , and were later repurposed by the New Republic .
History
Several years after the fall of the Imperion , while trapped in a cantina on the planet Nevarro by the forces of former Imperial Moff Gideon , the former Rebel Alliance shock trooper Cara Dune believed that she would be uploaded to a Mind Flayer if she surrendered. Greef Karga , agent of the Bounty Hunters' Guild , denied that Mind Flayers existed, believing them to be wartime propaganda , but Dune was not willing to find out whether he was correct.
Despite the prohibition of torture by the Galactic Concordance , the New Republic used a type of Mind Flayer, the Six-O-Two Mitigator . Doctor Penn Pershing was forced to go through a mental recalibration using lower voltages, an experimental process thought to help soothe traumatic memories to help one overcome Imperial indoctrination. However, this process was sabotaged by Elia Kane , who increased the voltage to its maximum, and wiped his mind.

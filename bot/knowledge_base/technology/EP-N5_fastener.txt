EP-N5 fasteners were a type of six-pronged panel joint assembled by the inmates of the Imperion 's Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex on the moon Narkina 5 . Complex in design and also identified as "widgets," the fasteners were then used in the construction of the Death Star superweapon 's Mk I Superlaser dish. The wider galaxy never learned of the joint system's true purpose, with escapee Cassian Jeron Andor only knowing it was a type of joint.
